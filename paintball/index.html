LEAGUE FORMAT

Six-player rosters. Three-player lineups.

Teams may register up to six players and select any three players from their roster for each weekly event. The active lineup may change for the next event, so teammates can rotate based on availability without leaving or changing teams.

Team roster

Each team registers three to six players and names one captain. The captain does not have to play or occupy a roster spot unless they register as a player.

Weekly event lineup

Teams choose three rostered players for each event. A different combination of three may represent the team at the following week's event.

Event lineup lock

A lineup change is allowed before the team's first match only if the replacement is ready immediately. Once that match begins, the lineup is locked through every round.

SEASON ROSTER MOVEMENT

Build teams without team hopping.

Adds & drops

Captain-managed rosters

Team captains may add or drop players between events while keeping their roster within the three-to-six-player limit.

Trades

Both captains must agree

Captains may trade players during the season only when both team captains approve the trade.

Team changes

No one-sided recruiting

A player may not leave one team for another during the season unless both the current captain and receiving captain agree.

OFFICIAL RULES

Know the rules before match day.

Season 1 uses 3v3 play, up-to-six-player team rosters, locked event lineups, captain-approved roster movement, ten-minute ready windows, fair-play requirements, and escalating penalties.

View Full Rulebook
