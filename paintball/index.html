HOW IT WORKS

Three steps from event signup to first point.

The two-step commitment gives organizers an early turnout estimate while ensuring teams use only players who are still available.

1. Register for an event

Browse the posted pickup nights and select Register for This Event whenever you find one you want to play.

2. Confirm attendance

Return during that event's confirmation window and press Confirm Attendance. Registration alone does not place you in the generator.

3. Get your team

After confirmation closes, MXS generates teams from confirmed players. Your assignment and the full schedule are then published online.
