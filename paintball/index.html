HOW IT WORKS

Three steps from signup to first point.

The format is built around actual attendance, so no absent captain or inactive permanent roster can prevent ready players from competing.

1. Register once

Sign in with Discord, enter your in-game name, and accept the rulebook. Your registration remains active for future pickup nights.

2. Check in online

For each night you want to play, return to the Player Portal during that event's displayed check-in window and confirm your attendance.

3. Get your team

After check-in closes, MXS generates the teams. Return to the portal for your assignment or open Tonight's Teams for the full lineup and schedule.

OFFICIAL RULES

Know the rules before event night.

Pickup nights use one-time player registration, event-night portal check-in, staff-formed teams, flexible attendance-based formats, fair-play requirements, and referee enforcement.
