LEAGUE FORMAT

Competition without rigid rosters.

Teams need three active players for a match and may register up to six. Players can move between teams between events, giving busy players room to compete without a season-long commitment.

Flexible teams

Minimum three players, maximum six, with only three playing at a time. Each team names one captain.

Event roster lock

Roster changes are welcome between tournaments. Once a team begins its first match, that event roster is locked.

Fair competition

A player may not compete for multiple teams in the same event. Staff rulings and sportsmanship rules apply equally to every roster.

OFFICIAL RULES

Know the rules before match day.

Season 1 uses 3v3 play, up-to-six-player event rosters, event roster locks, ten-minute ready windows, fair-play requirements, and escalating penalties for rule violations.
