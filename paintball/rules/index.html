INFINITE TOURNAMENT PAINTBALL

MXS Paintball Rulebook

Official rules for MXS 3v3 competition in Infinite Tournament Paintball.
Season 1 • Version 1.2 • August 2026

Back to PaintballQuestions? Join Discord
01

League overview & sportsmanship

MXS provides organized, competitive, and fair 3v3 tournament play in Infinite Tournament Paintball. Respect players and officials. Harassment, hate speech, excessive toxicity, cheating, exploiting, and intentional disruption are prohibited.

02

Teams, captains & rosters

Teams register a minimum of three and maximum of six players. Each team names a captain responsible for registration, roster updates, staff communication, and match issues. The captain may serve as a non-playing captain and does not count toward the three-to-six-player roster unless listed separately as a player. A captain who wants to compete must be registered on the team roster and selected for that event's lineup.

03

Event lineups, substitutions & lineup lock

Teams select any three players from their registered roster for each weekly event. The lineup may be changed before the team's first match begins, but any replacement player must be eligible and immediately ready to play. MXS will not delay a match or provide additional preparation time for a substitute. Once the team's first match begins, its three-player lineup is locked through every round of that event. An emergency substitution after the lineup locks requires league-staff approval. A player may not represent multiple teams in the same event.

04

Season roster changes & trades

Team captains may add or drop players between events as long as the roster remains within the three-to-six-player limit. A trade or any move from one team to another during the season requires approval from both the player's current team captain and the receiving team captain. Players may not switch teams without both captains agreeing. These rules are intended to prevent team hopping and one team from recruiting the strongest players away from other active rosters.

05

Season, tournament & match format

Season 1 consists of eight weekly rounds. Each round is one complete eight-team tournament containing 12 matches. Every team plays three matches. Opening-match winners enter the championship side and compete for first through fourth; opening-match losers enter the consolation side and compete for fifth through eighth. The second stage contains two championship semifinals and two consolation semifinals. The final stage contains matches for first, third, fifth, and seventh place, producing a clear first-through-eighth finish. Opening matches are race to 5 with a 10-minute limit. Semifinal and placement matches are race to 7 with a 15-minute limit. MXS may adjust the format before registration closes if attendance requires it.

06

Scoring, time limits & overtime

A point is earned by eliminating the opposing team or completing the active map objective. If time expires, the team with the higher score wins regardless of remaining body count. If the score is tied, one sudden-death overtime point is played on the same layout to determine the winner.

07

MXS scheduling, check-in, forfeits & disconnects

MXS sets the official event and match schedule. Teams have ten minutes after their scheduled match time to report ready or may forfeit. A substitute does not extend this deadline and must be ready to play immediately. A disconnect before meaningful gameplay may cause a restart; after play develops, officials determine whether play continues or the point is replayed.

08

Eliminated-player communication

After being eliminated, a player may only say “Dead,” “I'm dead,” “Traded,” “I've traded,” or “Out.” Eliminated players may not communicate positions, body counts, last-player information, or any other tactical information. Once all three players are eliminated and the point has ended, normal team communication may resume. In-game text chat is not allowed until the match is finished.

09

Official team voice channels

During an official match, every active player must remain in their assigned MXS Discord team voice channel and may not use a private call, separate Discord channel, or outside voice service. Each channel is limited to the team's three active players, one registered coach, and one MXS referee. Substitutes, spectators, and other team members may not join while the match is in progress. Eliminated players must follow the communication restrictions in Rule 08. Coaches may communicate between points but must remain silent while a point is live. Referees monitor team communications, and refusing to use the official channel makes a player ineligible to participate.

10

Gameplay restrictions

Hacks, scripts, exploits, glitches, bunny hopping, super crawling, jump shooting, shooting before the starting buzzer, and other unfair third-party or gameplay advantages are prohibited. Jumping is permitted only to cross beams. Players may not deliberately remain, run, or shoot from on top of a beam and must move off immediately after landing on one.

11

Penalties & enforcement

A minor penalty requires the offending player to sit out the next point, leaving the team to play 2v3. A major penalty requires two players to sit out the next point, leaving the team to play 1v3. If a team has no eligible players available, it automatically loses that point. The offending player must serve their penalty unless the referee directs otherwise. Repeated, deliberate, improperly served, or contested violations may result in a point loss, match forfeit, tournament disqualification, suspension, or permanent ban.

12

Referees, hosting, disputes & scoring corrections

Matches must be hosted by an MXS referee or approved neutral host, not a member of either competing team. Suspected infractions must be reported to the referee before the next point begins. Captains must raise disputes respectfully; arguing with officials or opposing teams may be penalized. Referees have final authority over rulings and may correct the official score when a verified game error records a point incorrectly.

13

Broadcast & community policy

MXS events may be streamed, recorded, clipped, and published. By participating, players agree that event gameplay may be used for broadcasts and league promotion. Racist insults, discrimination, threats, fighting, harassment, and abusive arguments are prohibited.

14

Championship standings & attendance

Each round awards championship points by final placement: first receives 100, second 90, third 80, fourth 70, fifth 60, sixth 50, seventh 40, and eighth 30. All eight rounds count toward the season championship; no round is dropped. A team must participate to earn placement points, and a team that misses a round receives zero points. A league-caused cancellation does not count as a completed round and will be rescheduled or removed equally for all teams.

15

Seeding, standings ties & rule changes

Round 1 seeding will be announced after registration closes. Later rounds are seeded from the current season standings unless MXS announces another method before that round. If teams are tied in championship points, the higher position goes to the team with the better finish in the most recent round; if still tied, compare event wins, then runner-up finishes, then third-place finishes. MXS may update these rules when needed, but major changes will not take effect during an active tournament.

MXS MISSION

Compete with integrity.

Our goal is a fun, competitive, and professional paintball community built on sportsmanship and respect.
