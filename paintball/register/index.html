Team Registration

Register a team for MXS 3v3 competition in Infinite Tournament Paintball. Team captains register three to six players and should review the roster rules before submitting.

MXS Paintball League is an independent, community-run competition and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the developer of Infinite Tournament Paintball.

BEFORE YOU START

Captain checklist

  • Choose a team name.
  • Have the captain's Discord username ready.
  • Register at least three and no more than six players.
  • The captain only needs to be listed as a player if they plan to compete.
  • Make sure players are not registered to another team.
  • Read and accept the official rulebook.

Only the team captain should submit this form. Roster changes and trades are handled under the season roster rules.
